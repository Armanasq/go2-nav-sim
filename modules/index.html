Deep dives

Each module derives one piece of a legged robot from first principles and checks it with code and measurements, on the Go2 in MuJoCo. No chapter depends on them. Modules are listed in dependency order.

  • validated: the fundamental level has a measured result here; each level below shows its own status
  • partial: used or explained here, or some levels done, but the fundamental level is not measured
  • planned: not started
  1. 00Mathematical foundationsvalidated
    • Fundamental Linear algebra, calculus, mechanics validated
    • Intermediate Coordinate transformations, quaternions, SO(3), SE(3) validated
    • Advanced Lie groups, numerical optimization partial
    Needs
    none
    In this repo
    modules/m00_math: rotations, quaternions, ZYX Euler extraction, SE(2)/SE(3), SO(3) exp/log, derived and matched to scipy and Pinocchio to ≤ 8e-15 over 100k samples (Pinocchio's log3 loses digits near π; the atan2 form here does not). Tilt-compensated yaw rate and the camera optical frame derived with numbers. Advanced level: SO(3) right/left Jacobians match central differences to 5e-10; Gauss-Newton and Levenberg-Marquardt written from scratch reach the closed-form SE(2) registration to 9e-15 / 6e-12 from every start on a 5° heading grid, and Cauchy IRLS with 20% wrong correspondences leaves 0.046° and 1.1 mm; FL leg calibration (3 encoder offsets, 2 link lengths) equals scipy to 4e-12 with mean NEES 4.64 for 5 parameters. 29 of 32 advanced checks pass. Missed: Jr vs Pinocchio Jexp3 5.2e-14 (> 1e-14) and Jr^-1 vs Pinocchio Jlog3 3.8e-10 (> 1e-10), both from Pinocchio losing digits below 0.01 rad; Gauss-Newton needs 17 iterations (> 10) from -145°, the start opposite the truth. 31 tests.
    chapter 9
    Next
    Close the three advanced misses or show they cannot be closed: compare Jexp3/Jlog3 below 0.01 rad against an extended-precision reference to decide which side loses the digits, and test whether a relative gradient tolerance removes the 9 rejected LM trials from 170°. Then extend the Jacobians to SE(3) for IMU preintegration in module 11 (state estimation).

    References

    • Lynch & Park, Modern Robotics (2017), ch. 3
    • Solà, Deray, Atchuthan, A micro Lie theory for state estimation in robotics (2018)

  5. 04Kinematicsvalidated
    • Fundamental Forward and inverse kinematics validated
    • Intermediate Jacobians, velocity kinematics, workspace validated
    • Advanced Differential IK, singularities, constrained IK partial
    Needs
    00, 02
    In this repo
    modules/m04_kinematics: FK, geometric Jacobian, numerical and closed-form IK from first principles. FK and Jacobian match MuJoCo and Pinocchio to ~1e-16; closed-form IK solves 100% of reachable targets to 7e-14 rad, 40–80× faster than the numerical solver (99.3–99.9%). det J = −l1·l2·sin(q3+γ)·s_z; the knee limits keep |sin(q3+γ)| ≥ 0.415. 20 tests.
    Next

  13. 12Perceptionpartial
    • Fundamental Cameras, depth sensors, LiDAR partial
    • Intermediate Point clouds, terrain reconstruction partial
    • Advanced Semantic perception, traversability estimation partial
    Needs
    06
    In this repo
    modules/m12_perception: depth camera (pinhole, Kinect axial noise) and a 16-beam LiDAR ray pattern on the Go2, both cast by an own slab ray caster that equals mj_multiRay to 1.6e-15 m on 244 800 camera rays and 4.4e-16 m on 63 360 LiDAR beams. Elevation map from 51 depth images: per-scan cell aggregation with a top-surface gate, scalar Kalman fusion of height and variance, consistent on constant-height cells (0.28 mm rms, mean NEES 0.995) and with per-scan pose errors when their Jacobian enters the variance (NEES 1.001; 4022 without it). Slope, roughness, step height, traversability and five terrain labels against the true height grid. 24 of 27 checks pass. Missed: coverage 0.776 (< 0.90, the course start and the upper stairs are out of view), traversability rms 0.074 (> 0.05, edges shifted by one cell), stairs recall 0.48 (< 0.80, the map flattens risers by 2 cm against an 8 cm threshold with 1.9 cm margin). LiDAR points are not mapped; the ROS 2 stack still uses a height-band scan.
    chapter 8 · chapter 10
    Next
    Fuse LiDAR points into the map to close the coverage gap; a variance term for the height change inside a cell on slopes; publish base_footprint to base_link from IMU roll/pitch and leg-kinematics height (module 04) in the ROS 2 stack.
