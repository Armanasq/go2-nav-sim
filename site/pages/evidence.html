What was run, and what came out

Everything on this page is generated when the site is built, from files in the repository: the metrics JSON of each run, the captured output of the missions, the unit tests and the site checks. If a number here disagrees with the repository, the build is out of date.

Missions

Three consecutive runs of scripts/run_mission.sh from a fresh start (new Gazebo, new estimator, new Nav2), on one machine (32 threads, RTX 4090 laptop GPU), with the final code. Metrics are computed by bag_metrics.py from the bag of each run.

{{RUNS_TABLE}}

All pairing is by message timestamp, with ground truth interpolated at the stamp of each estimate. ATE aligned: RMSE of the odometry position after the best rigid SE(2) alignment (Umeyama), the standard definition. ATE anchored: aligned on the first pose only, so drift shows. RPE: translational error over 1 m of travelled path. AMCL error is scored only at AMCL's own updates; an earlier version held the last AMCL pose between updates and overstated the error by about 2–3 cm. End distance to B: Nav2 stops inside its 0.25 m goal tolerance, so this number mostly reflects that setting. Ground truth initialises AMCL once and is never used again.

What broke, and what fixed it

Each of these cost time while building the stack. Chapter 12 has the full table.

Odometry turned half as fast as the robot

Symptom
During an in-place turn the estimate reached 36° while the robot had turned 65°.
Cause
IMU and lidar callbacks shared a thread; IMU samples that arrived during a registration fell outside the next integration window.
Fix
Integrate the gyro into a timestamped history and look it up by time; separate callback groups.
Proof
chapter 9, unit test test_gyro_yaw_is_independent_of_arrival_order

Scan matching flipped the room by 180°

Symptom
When Nav2's spin recovery turned the robot at 1 rad/s, odometry yaw ended 175° off.
Cause
A rectangular room is nearly symmetric; full SE(2) ICP can lock onto the flipped solution. The integrated gyro matched ground truth to 1–2°.
Fix
Yaw from the gyro, ICP solves translation only.
Proof
three of three missions afterwards

A uniform prior biased every pose

Symptom
Unit test: with the prediction 25 cm off, registration stopped 2.5 cm short; without the prior, 1–2 mm.
Cause
A prior added in every direction to stop sliding along walls also pulls well-constrained solutions.
Fix
Prior only along directions whose information eigenvalue is below 10 (arena: 90–100; corridor: 0).
Proof
test_corridor_prior_stops_sliding_but_corrects_across

The floor became a wall

Symptom
The global costmap filled with obstacles before the robot moved; the planner found no path.
Cause
base_footprint sits 0.209 m below the real ground (CHAMP publishes a constant 0.426 m base height), so a 0.12 m threshold was below the floor. I first blamed gait tilt; that came later in the analysis.
Fix
Height band 0.30–0.90 m in that frame (about 0.09–0.69 m real). The proper fix, a correct base_link transform from IMU tilt and leg kinematics, is open.
Proof
chapter 10

Sensors existed in Gazebo but not in ROS

Symptom
No /scan, no camera, no /imu/data, and no error anywhere.
Cause
ros-humble-gazebo-plugins was not installed and there was no sudo.
Fix
setup.sh unpacks the three packages into overlay/ with apt-get download.
Proof
chapter 12

Nav2 would not start on a busy machine

Symptom
change_state ... client will not receive response; lifecycle bring-up aborted.
Cause
Fast DDS dropped service replies under load; separately, orphaned lifecycle managers ignored SIGTERM and spun at 100% CPU each.
Fix
Cyclone DDS on loopback, a retrying up.sh, and SIGKILL escalation in stop.sh.
Proof
chapter 2

Limits of this evidence

  • Three runs on one machine is a small sample. Earlier versions of the estimator failed about one run in three; the causes are in chapter 9.
  • The arena is small and closed, so the odometry behaves partly like localisation against a fixed map.
  • AMCL starts from the true pose and the map is known in advance.
  • The dependency list in chapter 2 was not tested on a clean operating-system install.
